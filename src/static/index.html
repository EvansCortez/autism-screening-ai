Before you begin

Can this tell me whether I am autistic?

No. This project evaluates agreement with dataset screening labels. It has not been validated against independent clinical diagnoses.

Are these raw questionnaire answers?

No. The current demo accepts pre-scored binary item values. A respondent-facing questionnaire needs verified item wording and scoring.

What does a perfect score mean here?

The observed score threshold matches the labels in this dataset. That does not establish perfect diagnostic accuracy.

What does the model actually learn?

How much of the apparent ML performance is explained by reconstructing an AQ-score threshold, and what signal remains when questionnaire information is removed?

Adult dataset · grouped validation

Evidence loads from hash-verified committed experiment outputs.

Why removing the sum is insufficient

The ten individual items still contain the information needed to reconstruct their sum and its threshold. Removing all questionnaire information also requires removing the individual items and recorded result.

Interpret uncertainty carefully

Repeated cross-validation predictions share training data. Split variability and conditional bootstrap intervals are not validated generalization intervals. Repeats are not independent studies.

Evidence still needed

Independent diagnoses, participant identifiers, recruitment details, clinical external validation, and a verified contribution relative to prior work. Label generation and item encoding are not established by accessible documentation.

ASD-PREDICT / RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Research you can inspect

An undergraduate computer science project about what screening-data models learn.

Methods and provenance

The adult UCI dataset contains ten scored items and YES/NO screening labels. The experiments compare simple scoring rules with machine learning, using training-fold preprocessing and duplicate-group-aware validation. They do not establish how labels were generated or whether they represent independent diagnoses.

Reproduce the analysis

Use the research checkout and its pinned Python 3.14.5 environment:

python3.14 scripts/reproduce.py

The UI does not retrain models or rewrite experiment results. Its research evidence version is recorded in src/static/research-version.json.

What this project does not claim

It is not a diagnostic product or a clinical service. Notes are supplied text, not validated diagnostic findings. No LLM, video, speech or computer-vision analysis is performed by this workflow. A finding about demographic fields does not establish why that association exists.
