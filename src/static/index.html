Adult AQ-10 Demo

Autism Screening Assistant Demo

This prototype turns adult AQ-10 questionnaire responses, supporting notes, and workflow fields into a risk estimate and a clinician-facing summary. It is built for screening support, not diagnosis.

How teams could use this

A respondent or intake staff member completes the AQ-style form, documents relevant observations, adds short notes, and generates a structured report for clinical follow-up.

Startup MVP direction

Start with an honest adult screening workflow demo, then layer on richer multimodal AI only after collecting the right data and review loops.
